Dark Age HQ

Dark Age HQ is a private desktop app for Windows that helps a tattoo shop owner run payroll and keep the shop's books. It runs on the owner's own computer.

With the owner's permission, Dark Age HQ can connect to the shop's QuickBooks Online company to read company-card spending, expenses and other accounting records, so they show up in the app's books and reports. It never changes anything in QuickBooks.

How to connect QuickBooks

Open Dark Age HQ on your computer, go to Settings → Connections → QuickBooks, and click Connect. You'll sign in to QuickBooks and approve access.
