Connect QuickBooks

Dark Age HQ is a desktop app, so QuickBooks is connected from inside the app.

  1. Open Dark Age HQ on your computer.
  2. Go to Settings → Connections → QuickBooks.
  3. Click Connect (or Sign in again), sign in to QuickBooks, and approve access.
