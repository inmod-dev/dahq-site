Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what Dark Age HQ ("the app") does with information. The app is made by Greysen Fox, doing business as iNMod. ("we", "us").

1. Where your data lives

The app runs on your own computer. Your business data, including data read from QuickBooks, is stored on that computer (and on any computer you choose to run it on). We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not have access to it.

2. What the app reads from QuickBooks

Only with your permission, the app reads accounting records from your QuickBooks Online company, such as company information, purchases, expenses and card or bank transactions, so they can appear in the app's books and reports. The app asks only for QuickBooks accounting access and uses it read-only: it does not create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks.

3. Sign-in information

When you connect QuickBooks, Intuit gives the app access tokens. The app stores them encrypted on your computer and uses them only to read your QuickBooks data. We never see your QuickBooks password. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Connections, which removes the tokens from your computer. You can also remove the app's access from your Intuit account.

4. What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not send your data to us or to other companies. The app's AI features run on your own computer.

5. Other services

When the app talks to QuickBooks, Intuit's own privacy policy applies to the data Intuit holds. Other services you choose to connect in the app have their own policies too.

6. Keeping data safe

Tokens are encrypted on your computer and kept out of the app's updates. Please keep your computer protected with a password and up-to-date security software.

7. Deleting data

Disconnecting QuickBooks removes its tokens. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything the app stored on that computer.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
