Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how Dark Age HQ ("the app") handles information. The app is made by Greysen Fox, operating as iNMod. ("we", "us"). The business that installs and uses the app is "you".

1. Where your data lives

The app runs on your own computer. Your business data, including data the app reads from services you connect, is stored on that computer (or on the computers you choose to run it on). We do not run servers that receive your data, and we cannot see it.

2. What the app handles

  • Your business records: sales, payroll, expenses and notes you enter or import, which can include names of your staff and clients and the amounts paid.
  • QuickBooks Online (only if you connect it): accounting records such as company information, purchases, expenses and card or bank transactions. The app asks only for QuickBooks accounting access and uses it to read: it does not create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks.
  • Other services you connect (for example your online forms or card processor): the records needed to show sales and payroll in the app.

You decide what to connect and what to enter. You are responsible for having the right to use your staff's and clients' information this way.

3. Sign-in information

When you connect QuickBooks, Intuit gives the app access tokens. The app stores them encrypted on your computer and uses them only to read your QuickBooks data. We never see your QuickBooks password. When you disconnect in Settings → Connections, the app asks Intuit to cancel its access and deletes the tokens from your computer. You can also remove the app's access from your Intuit account at any time.

4. What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not send your data to us. The app's AI features run on your own computer; your data only travels between your computer and the services you connect.

5. Other companies' policies

Intuit (QuickBooks) and any other service you connect have their own privacy policies, which apply to the data they hold.

6. Keeping data safe

Sign-in tokens are encrypted on your computer and are never included in the app's updates. Please protect your computer with a password, keep it updated, and keep backups.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. Disconnecting a service deletes its tokens. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything the app stored on that computer. Data already held by QuickBooks or other services stays with them.

8. Children

The app is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: contact@inmod.dev
